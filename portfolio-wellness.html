Project Description

Designed a cohesive suite of print and digital promotional materials for the University of Colorado Denver Wellness Center to increase student engagement and event participation. Spanning initiatives like group fitness schedules, Mental Health First Aid courses, and social media campaigns, the project involved end-to-end design execution from stakeholder research and conceptualization to final layout and typography. By incorporating clear visual hierarchies, consistent branding, and scannable QR codes, the final assets effectively communicated essential wellness resources across both physical on-campus displays and digital platforms.
