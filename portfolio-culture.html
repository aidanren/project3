Project Description

Led the comprehensive brand identity and digital design for New to Culture (NTC), a clothing brand utilizing tote bags as canvases for cultural storytelling. The project encompassed end-to-end visual identity creation, including a versatile logo system, contemporary typography, and a curated color palette. This identity was then seamlessly translated into a minimal, narrative-driven web experience. Through strategic research, wireframing, and high-fidelity UI design, the final digital presence effectively balances commerce with storytelling, ensuring a cohesive and engaging user experience across both physical products and online touchpoints.
